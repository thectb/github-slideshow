Clinical Recommendations

Prioritized Interventions
Vitamin D Deficiency — Immediate Supplementation Required
Urgent

Vitamin D level at 22 ng/mL is significantly deficient (minimum target: 30 ng/mL, optimal: 50-70 ng/mL). This deficiency can impair immune function, bone density, mood regulation, cardiovascular health, and metabolic optimization. Recommend immediate high-dose supplementation protocol: 10,000 IU daily for 8 weeks to rapidly correct deficiency, then transition to maintenance dose of 4,000-5,000 IU daily. Take with fat-containing meal for optimal absorption. Consider testing for malabsorption issues if levels fail to respond. Recheck 25-hydroxyvitamin D level in 8-12 weeks to confirm adequacy. Given your otherwise excellent health, correcting this single deficiency will further optimize overall wellness and performance.
