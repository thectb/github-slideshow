Clinical Recommendations

Prioritized Interventions
Cardiovascular Risk — Immediate Dietary Intervention
Urgent

LDL cholesterol at 142 mg/dL and triglycerides at 186 mg/dL indicate moderate cardiovascular risk. Recommend strict reduction of saturated fat and refined carbohydrates. Initiate omega-3 supplementation at 3 g/day. Increase aerobic exercise to 150 min/week minimum. Consider adding plant sterols and soluble fiber. Re-test in 60 days. If no improvement, statin therapy may be indicated.

Glucose Control — Early Insulin Resistance Detected
Priority

HbA1c at 5.6% is borderline and suggests early insulin resistance, particularly given elevated triglycerides. Implement low-glycemic diet, eliminate added sugars, and consider time-restricted eating (16:8 protocol). Add resistance training 3x/week. Monitor fasting glucose weekly. Berberine or metformin may be considered if lifestyle changes insufficient.

Vitamin D — Optimization Recommended
Maintenance

Current level at 42 ng/mL is adequate but not optimal. Increase supplementation to 4,000–5,000 IU daily to target 60–70 ng/mL for optimal immune and metabolic function.

Hormonal Status — Continue Current Protocol

Testosterone and IGF-1 levels are strong. Thyroid function optimal. No modification indicated. Annual monitoring sufficient.
